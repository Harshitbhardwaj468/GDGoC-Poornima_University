Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and contributing to GDG on Campus Poornima.

Is GDG membership open to all departments or only CSE?

expand_more
GDG on Campus Poornima University welcomes students from any engineering branch, BCA, MCA, design, and management. As long as you have a passion for technology, problem-solving, and community collaboration, you belong here.

Do I have to pay any membership or registration fee?

expand_more
Never. GDG on Campus is completely free. All our workshops, hackathons, cloud learning platforms, and codelabs are sponsored by Google Developers and Poornima University.

What if I have zero coding experience right now?

expand_more
You are our primary audience! We host beginner bootcamps (Git 101, Python Basics, Web Foundations) specifically designed to kickstart your journey. Many of our past core team members started with zero line commits.

What is the Google Solution Challenge?

expand_more
It is an annual global competition that invites university students to develop solutions for one or more of the United Nations 17 Sustainable Development Goals using Google products or platforms. Top teams receive cash awards, global Google mentoring, and recognition.

How do I become a Core Team organizer?

expand_more
Core Team recruitment cycles take place every year before the academic term starts. Active community volunteers, workshop facilitators, and project contributors get priority consideration during the application process.
