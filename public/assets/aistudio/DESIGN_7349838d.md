## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tech, welcoming, and developer-first design language tailored for university technocrats, student developers, and open-source contributors. The visual ethos merges the iconic, primary-rich Google developer identity with a refined, contemporary product design baseline. 

The aesthetic is clean, technical, and approachable:
- **Aesthetic Classification:** Modern Developer Utility with Material-inspired tonal structure and crisp structural framing.
- **Atmosphere:** Optimistic, engineering-focused, collaborative, and precise.
- **Visual Weight:** Light, breathable surfaces balanced by purposeful accents of signature Google primary hues, avoiding sensory overload through strict 60-30-10 distribution rules (neutrals dominate, primaries guide focus).
- **Core Pillars:** Code-level legibility, accessible contrast benchmarks (WCAG 2.1 AAA on core copy, AA on graphic accents), and tactile affordances for cards, badges, and interactive developer terminals.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the Google developer palette while anchoring them against high-performance slate neutrals suited for technical dashboards, event portals, and code documentation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#4285F4` - Google Blue):** Dominates primary interactive states, links, main navigation highlights, focus outlines, and brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#34A853` - Google Green):** Designates success states, hackathon submission confirmations, active statuses, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#EA4335` - Google Red):** Handles urgent notifications, live event alerts, destructive developer actions, and high-impact key visuals.
- **Accent Warning (`#FBBC04` / `#F9AB00` - Google Yellow):** Reserved for badges, stars, work-in-progress sprints, and spotlight tags. Must utilize `#B06000` or neutral slate text overlay for guaranteed contrast compliance on light mode.

### Neutral Layering
- **Light Theme (Default):** Canvas begins at `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50), building upward to `#FFFFFF` (Surface-0), framed by subtle `#E2E8F0` (Slate-200) structural borders. Base typography relies on `#0F172A` (Slate-900) for primary text and `#64748B` (Slate-500) for metadata.
- **Dark Theme (Automatic / Toggled):** Base canvas drops to `#0B0F19` with elevated card containers at `#0F172A` (Slate-900) and elevated nested surfaces at `#1E293B` (Slate-800). Borders transition to `#334155` (Slate-700 / 40% alpha). Primary brand accents adjust slightly in luminance to maintain perceptual parity without harsh vibrance shifts.

## Typography

The typographical pairing solves for two distinct needs: geometric brand friendliness and rigorous technical scanning.

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric structure, approachable curves, and close affinity with modern developer product design. It injects energy into event headlines, track announcements, and section titles.
- **Interface & Long-form Body (Inter):** Serves as the primary workhorse across UI controls, workshop summaries, speaker bios, and documentation. Delivers neutral balance and high rendering legibility across micro screens and desktop monitors.
- **Developer Metas & Code Blocks (JetBrains Mono):** Leveraged for tags, terminal windows, API snippets, timestamps, badge counts, and workshop tracks (e.g., `cloud/devops`, `web/flutter`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an adaptable 8-point rhythmic grid, transitioning to a strict 4-point sub-grid for internal component layout (inputs, chips, badges).

### Grid Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid. Outer margins are anchored at `2.5rem` to `3rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum reading content shell constrains at `1280px` to maintain optimal line widths.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` canvas margins.
- **Mobile (Below 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Complex developer cards (e.g., dual-speaker schedules, repository metrics) stack vertically into full-width cards.

### Layout Rhythms
Sections utilize spacious top/bottom padding (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` on desktops) to let white space emphasize primary color accents. Dense UI surfaces like developer dashboards and event agendas rely on compact modular padding (`space-md`) to ensure dense utility without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy skeuomorphic drop-shadows in favor of crisp tonal tiering paired with low-opacity ambient diffusion.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Light Mode: `#F8FAFC`; Dark Mode: `#0B0F19`. Zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards, Panels):** Light Mode: `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-soft shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Dark Mode: `#0F172A` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Interactive Focus):** Elevated with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. When hovered, cards subtly tint their border with the corresponding track color (Google Blue for dev tracks, Green for design tracks, Yellow for cloud tracks, Red for AI tracks).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs, Floating Navigation):** High-level overlay with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Supported by a backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` scrim).

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded architecture (Option 2). This standard reflects modern Android and developer portal components: friendly and approachable, yet structural and disciplined.

- **Micro Controls (Inputs, Badges, Small Buttons):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / `8px`).
- **Cards, Panels & Code Blocks:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg` / `16px`).
- **Major Containers & Hero Callouts:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` / `24px`).
- **Interactive Badges & Pill Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) reserved specifically for single-line contextual status indicators (e.g., `RSVP Open`, `Live Now`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#4285F4` background with pure white typography (`Inter` Medium). Hover state shifts to `#3367D6` with a micro-scale of 1.01. Focus ring displays a 2px offset border in `#4285F4`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1px border in `#CBD5E1` (`#334155` in dark mode), neutral text `#0F172A` (`#F8FAFC` in dark mode). Hover triggers a subtle background wash of `rgba(66, 133, 244, 0.06)`.
- **Primary Google Tone Alternates:** Specific campus campaigns can adopt Green (`#34A853`) for registration confirmation buttons or Red (`#EA4335`) for live stream entry.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Surface at `#FFFFFF` (Dark: `#0F172A`) enclosed in a 1px border `#E2E8F0` (Dark: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Track Accent Variant:** A 3px colored accent bar or top edge rendered in one of the 4 signature colors indicating the event domain (Blue = Mobile/Web, Green = Sustainability/Cloud, Red = AI/ML, Yellow = Security/Foundations).

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag Chips:** Monospace typography (`JetBrains Mono`, `code-sm`), pill-rounded (`9999px`), internal padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Tonal Color Coding:**
  - *Blue Chip:* Background `rgba(66, 133, 244, 0.1)`, Text `#1A73E8`
  - *Green Chip:* Background `rgba(52, 168, 83, 0.1)`, Text `#1E8E3E`
  - *Red Chip:* Background `rgba(234, 67, 53, 0.1)`, Text `#D93025`
  - *Yellow Chip:* Background `rgba(251, 188, 4, 0.15)`, Text `#B06000`

### Input Fields
- Flat container with `0.5rem` corner radius, `#FFFFFF` background (Dark: `#1E293B`), 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state eliminates the native browser ring and implements a 2px solid border in `#4285F4` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(66, 133, 244, 0.15)` focus glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 18px controls with rounded corners (4px for checkboxes, 50% for radio buttons).
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`.
- Checked: Solid `#4285F4` fill with crisp white vector checkmark/dot and smooth 150ms spring transitions.

### Developer-Specific Additions
- **Terminal Snippet Box:** Matte Slate-950 (`#020617`) background container, 16px radius, featuring mock macOS/Linux title bar controls (red, yellow, green micro dots), syntax highlighting, and a one-click copy button in the top right.
- **Speaker & Contributor Avatars:** Circular avatar rings sporting a 2px offset border rotating sequentially across the four Google brand colors.
